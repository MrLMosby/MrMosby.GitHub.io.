Data & Methods

This site displays supplied risk scores; it does not calculate an AI replacement model. A score of 80 does not mean an 80% chance of job loss. Display bands: Low <40, Moderate 40–<70, High ≥70. These are display conventions, not validated forecasting thresholds.

When all filtered records have employment and total employment is positive, averages use sum(score × employment) / sum(employment). Otherwise, the dashboard uses an unweighted record average. Missing employment is not treated as zero in completeness checks.

Import mutually exclusive occupation/location records. Do not combine national totals, state totals, metro totals, and detailed records that overlap. Prepare data joins before importing. Add your data sources, versions, score formula, weights, and limitations here.

Imports remain in this browser tab only. Reloading resets the page to sample data. Use aggregate, nonconfidential data. No data is uploaded to a server by this file.
